Reading it

The bright rope is the route from the current cell back to the start — for a stack-based search that is exactly what the frontier would have to unwind through. When it snaps backwards, that is a backtrack.

The blue band is every cell pushed but not yet popped. Watch how wide it gets: that is what max frontier measures, and it is the memory your search actually costs. When the goal is reached the trail dims and the route it settled on is drawn in green.
